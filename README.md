# Life Brain

Tasks, calendar, notes, habits and goals in one simple app, with an optional AI that looks for patterns. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account and no server.

## Adding things

The **+** button at the bottom right of Home, Today and Calendar adds anything from one sheet.

With an AI key set up, **Auto** is picked first. Type it however it comes ("dentist friday 3pm", "gym every morning", "read 12 books this year") and press Enter. Your AI decides whether it's a task, habit, goal, note or calendar event, and it's added straight away in the right place; the screen updates at once and one Undo takes it back. A time you typed ("3pm") becomes the event's time. If the AI's answer can't be used, nothing is added and your text stays in the box. Offline, + starts on Task instead. For a long brain dump with many things in it, write a note and use Organise.

Or pick Task, Event, Note, Habit or Goal yourself, type, then tap a chip: Today, Tomorrow, Weekend, Next week, No date, or Other day for any date. Tasks and habits have goal chips too. Press Enter or tap Add. A date word at the end still works ("Call mom friday"), and an event reads its time ("Dentist 3:30pm"). A goal needs only its name and opens straight at its first step. On Goals and Notes, + adds a goal or a note.

Swipe a task right to tick it off, or left to move it to tomorrow. Both have Undo, and the circle and the task sheet do the same.

## The six screens

Everything is in the menu: tap **☰** at the top left, or swipe in from the left edge. Each item says what it's for, and Settings is at the bottom. The first time you open the app, **How it works** explains each screen. It's in the menu too, whenever you want it again.

- **Home.** A 30-second look at the day, opened first. One line sums up the day. The calendar is drawn as a line that rises where the day is fuller: a sun marks open time, and a dot in the logo's colours marks now. Today's events sit under it as chips. Then, in this order:
  - **Where you're heading.** One sentence on the thing most worth knowing about your goals: a goal that will miss its date at this pace, one that hasn't moved in 10 days or more, or how much of your work isn't toward any goal. Under it, a bar showing how the tasks you finished in the last 4 weeks split across your goals, and a row for each of your first three goals with its state. Tap a row to open the goal. With no goals yet, it asks for one.
  - **Fixes for you.** Cards side by side; swipe through them. Each is one specific change you can apply in one tap, with Undo: put a stalled goal's next step on tomorrow, or move the newest loose tasks off a day that's fuller than you usually manage (tasks tied to goals stay). With AI set up, a card can also plan steps for a goal that has none, plan the week when a goal is behind, break down a task you keep moving, tie loose tasks to goals, or write the weekly look back. A backup reminder shows here when one is due, and saves the file in one tap. **Not now** hides a card for a week. Suggestions your AI prepared by itself come first.
  - **Today.** Overdue and today's tasks, ticked right here, and your habits as chips you tap to tick. The rest is on the Today screen.
  - **Coming up** (tomorrow's events and notes left for today or tomorrow) and **Already done** (tasks finished, habits kept, goals reached).

  Add your name in Settings and the headline uses it.
- **Today.** Today's tasks: type to add one, tap the circle (or swipe) to tick it off. Undated tasks sit under *Anytime*. When something is overdue, a bar offers **Sort out** (AI) or **Move to today**. Below are your habits. Type a date word at the end, like "Call mom tomorrow" or "Pay rent friday", and the task lands on that day.
- **Calendar.** The month. Tap a day to see and add its tasks and events, and to open notes written that day. Swipe sideways to change month. The + button adds to the day you're looking at.
- **Goals.** What you want, why, and by when. Only the name is needed. A goal can count something (12 books) or be finished by its steps (Finish Paper 2). Adding one takes you straight to its first step, and steps you add there are tasks tied to the goal. Each goal shows how far along it is and whether it's on track, behind its date at the current pace, stalled, or missing a next step. Tasks and habits are tied to a goal with one tap on its chip in their own sheet. With no goals yet, the screen has a few examples to start from.
- **Notes.** For keeping track of why things happened: decisions, reasons, what worked. Each note belongs to a day, today unless you pick another (there's a Tomorrow chip). It shows on that day in Calendar, and Home brings up notes you left for today or tomorrow. Search and pin them.
- **Organise a note.** Brain-dump in a note, then tap **Organise**. The AI reads that one note and suggests tasks (with dates), events (with times), habits and goals, and ties each task or habit to the goal it serves. You see exactly what is sent first, then check the list: untick, retitle, change a date or a kind. **Add** puts each item where it belongs, and Undo takes them all back. Items you already have start unticked. The note stays as the reason behind them.
- **Progress.** The last 7 days in numbers, your habits over 4 weeks, and **What you might be missing**: plain sentences the app finds in your own records, like a habit that goes with better days, one that slipped, or a task you keep moving. **Ask what it sees** sends the last month to an AI of your choice. You see exactly what will be sent first, and notes are left out unless you tick them.

The look comes from the logo: plum ink, violet for buttons and ticks, and the logo's violet-to-pink gradient only where your AI is involved (every ✦ button) and on the "now" dot on Home. Page titles use Bricolage Grotesque; everything else uses Inter. Choices are chips you tap rather than dropdowns, and the sheets slide up from the bottom with their buttons always in reach. Dark is true black.

Settings (last in the menu) has your name, the theme (Auto, Light, Dark), the accent colour (violet unless you pick another), the AI key, and backups.

## AI

Every ✦ button works in one tap: it asks your AI straight away and shows the answer. Anything it suggests changing is listed with tick boxes, so nothing changes until you tap Apply or Add, and one Undo reverses the lot. Each sheet has **See what is sent** if you want to check exactly what left the phone. With Automatic on, a button opens the suggestion your AI already prepared instead of asking again. When you have goals, each request includes every goal's reason, date and current state, so the answers are about what you said you want.

- **Auto** (in +): one thing you typed, sorted into a task, habit, goal, note or calendar event and added at once, with Undo.
- **Organise** (in a note): turns a brain dump into tasks, events, habits and goals, tied to your goals.
- **Plan steps** (in a goal, or a fix on Home): 3 to 6 next steps toward the goal, never past its date.
- **Tie to goals** (a fix on Home): matches your open tasks to the goals they serve, so Where you're heading is accurate.
- **Break down** (in a task): 3 to 6 small steps with dates, never past the task's due date. It can replace the big task, and the steps keep its goal.
- **Plan week** (top of Today): spreads open tasks over the next 7 days, sized to how many you actually finish a day, with steps for goals that are behind first.
- **Sort out** (the overdue bar on Today): do today, move, split or delete, for each one.
- **What matters today?** (Home): one sentence about today under the headline, kept until midnight.
- **Weekly look back** (Progress, and a card on Home on Sundays and Mondays): done, slipped, one thing to try. Save it as a note.
- **Ask** (Notes): ask a question and get an answer from your notes, with links to the notes it used.
- **Ask what it sees** (Progress): patterns in your last month.

**Automatic** (Settings, off until you switch it on): the AI does its thinking by itself while the app is open and online. That covers a line about today on your first open each day, ideas for overdue tasks once a day when two or more are overdue, organising a note when you close it, a weekly look back on Sunday or Monday (saved to Notes), and a plan for the week on Mondays. You can switch each one off on its own. Anything that would change your tasks waits on Home under Fixes for you as a "✦" item until you open it and tap Apply, or Not now. It sends the same things the buttons send. Settings shows exactly what the last automatic run sent, and any failure. It needs **Keep keys on this phone** on, or it stops when the app closes.

Paste any API key in Settings → AI. The app recognises the service (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Perplexity, Together, Fireworks, Cerebras, Hugging Face, or your own OpenAI-compatible server), checks the key for free and picks a model. Keys stay in memory unless you tick *Keep keys on this phone*. They are never put in backups.

## Your data

- Stored in this browser on this phone (IndexedDB). A backup is made on the phone each day you open the app.
- **Export backup** saves a file. Keep one somewhere else now and then. **Import backup** reads it back. Backups from the earlier version of Life Brain are converted.
- The first time it opens, the app copies your own items from the earlier version (tasks, events, habits, memories as notes, day notes as dated notes). Entries from the old journal also become dated notes. It leaves the example data behind and doesn't change the old data.

## Install

Open https://akashdeshmukh-git.github.io/life-brain/ in Chrome on Android, then menu (⋮) → **Install app**. It works offline after the first open.

## Rebuild and test

The app is one file: `index.html` holds the markup, the script, the styles and the fonts, so it opens offline and needs nothing from the internet. `sw.js`, the manifest and the icons sit next to it so it can be installed.

The styles are written with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com) and compiled into the page when it's built, not loaded from a CDN. That keeps the single file working offline and keeps the page's strict security policy (no outside scripts).

Inside `source`: `npm install` once (Tailwind, PostCSS and the display font), then `node build.mjs` writes `dist/pwa`. `python3 tests/run.py` runs the browser tests (needs Python Playwright with Chromium).

- `src/tw.css`: the colour tokens (light and dark), the shared pieces (buttons, chips, fields, sheets) and a few drawing rules.
- `tailwind.config.js`: maps Tailwind colour names to those tokens, so the accent picker and dark mode change them at runtime.
- `src/js/*.js`: the app, joined in name order.

Inter and Bricolage Grotesque are free fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Their licences are in `licenses/`.
